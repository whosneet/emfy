Emfy — Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Emfy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no analytics, no accounts, and no ability to make network connections. This is true on Mac, iPhone and iPad; Emfy's App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

1 — What Emfy collects

Nothing. Emfy contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising identifiers, or usage tracking of any kind, and it never asks you to create an account.

2 — Your files

EMF and WMF files you open, preview, or export are processed entirely on your device, whether that is a Mac, an iPhone or an iPad. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared. The app and its Quick Look extensions run sandboxed and are built without a network entitlement — the operating system prevents them from making network connections at all.

3 — Quick Look previews and thumbnails

When Finder on the Mac, or Files, Mail or Messages on iPhone and iPad, shows a preview or thumbnail of a metafile, the operating system invokes Emfy's sandboxed extensions on your device. The rendering happens locally and the file's contents never leave your device.

4 — Photos (iPhone and iPad)

On iPhone and iPad, when you choose “Save to Photos” for a picture you have exported, Emfy asks the system for add-only access to your photo library so it can save that one image. This is the only reason Emfy touches Photos: it only adds the picture you asked it to save, and it never reads, browses, or lists the photos already in your library.

5 — Third-party services

Emfy uses no third-party services, SDKs, or dependencies. There is nothing in the app that could share data with anyone.

6 — App Store distribution

If you install Emfy from the App Store, Apple may collect standard download and diagnostics information according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policy. That collection is controlled by Apple, not by Emfy.

7 — Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

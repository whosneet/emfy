Emfy — Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

Emfy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no analytics, no accounts, and no advertising. This is true on Mac, iPhone and iPad; Emfy's App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

The only times Emfy uses the network are ones you start yourself: if you configure your own AI provider (see “AI features” below), and, in the version downloaded from GitHub, when it checks GitHub for an update. Emfy has no servers of its own, and Arrley receives nothing in either case.

1 — What Emfy collects

Nothing. Emfy contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising identifiers, or usage tracking of any kind, and it never asks you to create an account.

2 — Your files

The metafiles you open, preview, edit, create, or export — EMF, WMF, and their compressed .emz and .wmz forms — are processed entirely on your device, whether that is a Mac, an iPhone or an iPad. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared, except to an AI provider you configure yourself, as described below.

3 — Quick Look previews and thumbnails

When Finder on the Mac, or Files, Mail or Messages on iPhone and iPad, shows a preview or thumbnail of a metafile, the operating system invokes Emfy's sandboxed extensions on your device. The rendering happens locally, the extensions have no network access, and the file's contents never leave your device.

4 — Photos (iPhone and iPad)

On iPhone and iPad, when you choose “Save to Photos” for a picture you have exported, Emfy asks the system for add-only access to your photo library so it can save that one image. This is the only reason Emfy touches Photos: it only adds the picture you asked it to save, and it never reads, browses, or lists the photos already in your library.

5 — AI features

Emfy's AI features — such as Describe, Ask, titles and tags, the accessibility Check, Compare, Recolour, Translate, Edit with AI, and New Drawing from a description — work on your device by default, using Apple Intelligence where your device supports it. Nothing about your drawings is sent anywhere in this mode.

You may also, entirely optionally, configure your own third-party AI provider (such as Anthropic, OpenAI, or an OpenAI-compatible or local model you run yourself). If you do:

  • Emfy asks for your explicit permission per provider before anything is ever sent, and shows you what will be sent and what will not.
  • Only then, and only for the feature you use, does Emfy send that provider the drawing content that feature needs — which can include the drawing's extracted text and, for features such as Describe or Edit with AI, a rendered image of the drawing — along with your request.
  • Your provider API key is stored only in the Keychain on your device. It is sent to no one but that provider, to authenticate.
  • The connection is directly between your device and the provider you chose. Emfy and Arrley run no servers and receive none of this data. Once the data reaches your provider, that provider's own privacy policy and terms govern it.
  • Drawing content is untrusted input: Emfy never lets a model take an action on your files without your confirmation.

6 — Updates (Mac direct-download version only)

If you install Emfy from GitHub rather than the App Store, that version can check github.com for updates. It fetches only GitHub's public release metadata; it sends no personal data and involves no Arrley servers. The App Store version updates through the App Store and does not include this updater.

7 — MCP server (Mac direct-download version only)

The Mac direct-download version includes an optional, off-by-default MCP server so AI tools you run on your own Mac can read your drawings. It runs locally over standard input and output (stdio) on your machine only — it has no network listener — and it is read-only. It never leaves your Mac unless the tool you connect to it sends data onward, which is governed by that tool, not by Emfy.

8 — Third-party services

Emfy bundles no third-party services, SDKs, advertising, or analytics. Nothing in the app shares your data with anyone on its own. The only external services involved are ones you choose: an AI provider you configure, and — for the GitHub version — GitHub for update checks.

9 — App Store distribution

If you install Emfy from the App Store, Apple may collect standard download and diagnostics information according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policy. That collection is controlled by Apple, not by Emfy.

10 — Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

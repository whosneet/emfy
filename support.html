Emfy — Support

Get help with Emfy, the free open-source app for viewing, editing and creating metafiles on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Email: hello@arrley.com — include the file if you can; a real file beats any description.

Issues: github.com/whosneet/emfy/issues — bug reports and rendering problems.

Which devices does Emfy support?

macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later, and iPhone and iPad on iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. It's one universal purchase across Mac, iPhone and iPad.

Which files does Emfy open?

EMF (.emf) and WMF (.wmf) files, including ones that use EMF+ records, plus the gzip-compressed .emz and .wmz forms. Emfy can also edit and save these formats, and export to PNG, PDF and SVG.

Previews or thumbnails aren't showing

On the Mac, launch the Emfy app once — macOS registers the Quick Look extensions from then on. If Finder still shows generic icons afterwards, log out and back in, and check that Emfy's extensions are enabled in System Settings (search for “Extensions”, then look under Quick Look). On iPhone and iPad, previews and thumbnails appear in Files, Mail and Messages once the app is installed.

A file renders wrongly or partially

Emfy renders what it can and lists anything it had to approximate or skip. A few files store some of their drawing content in formats Emfy simplifies. For anything that looks wrong, email the file or open an issue and it becomes a test case for the next release.

Do the AI features send my drawings anywhere?

By default, no. Emfy's AI features run on your device using Apple Intelligence where your device supports it. If you choose to set up your own AI provider, Emfy sends drawing content only after you give explicit, per-provider consent, and only directly to the provider you configured, under that provider's own terms. Your API key is kept in the Keychain, and Arrley receives nothing. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where do I get the latest version?

On the Mac, the latest release on GitHub always has the newest notarized Emfy.dmg, and the same app is on the App Store for Mac, iPhone and iPad.
